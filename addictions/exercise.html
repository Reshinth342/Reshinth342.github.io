Healthy Movement vs Compulsive Exercise

Exercise addiction occurs when physical activity transforms into a rigid emotional reliance, pursued despite acute physical injury, illness, or severe disruption to social and family life.

Common Warning Signs

  • Continuing intense workouts despite joint injury, illness, or medical advice.
  • Experiencing extreme anxiety, depression, or guilt when a workout is missed.
  • Exercising compulsively to compensate for eating food.
  • Structuring entire days around workout schedules at the expense of family/work.

🌿 Building a Balanced Relationship with Exercise

Incorporate mandatory rest days, focus on joyful low-impact movement (walking, gentle yoga), and reframe physical activity as restorative rather than compensatory.
