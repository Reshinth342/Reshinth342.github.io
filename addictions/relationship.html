Compulsive Relationship Patterns

Relationship addiction involves an obsessive reliance on romantic validation or remaining in toxic, destructive relationship dynamics due to an intense, overwhelming fear of solitude.

Common Warning Signs

  • Feeling complete panic, emptiness, or loss of identity when not in a romantic relationship.
  • Rushing into intense relationships without evaluating personal safety, values, or fit.
  • Tolerating mistreatment or emotional abuse to avoid being alone.
  • Obsessive tracking or controlling behavior directed toward romantic partners.
