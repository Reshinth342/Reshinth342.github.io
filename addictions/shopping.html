Compulsive Buying Disorder

Compulsive buying involves an uncontrollable urge to purchase unneeded items to soothe negative feelings (anxiety, sadness, low self-worth), resulting in severe financial and emotional consequences.

Common Warning Signs

  • Shopping as the primary mechanism to handle stress, anger, or sadness.
  • Accumulating unopened packages or hiding purchases from family members.
  • Relying heavily on buy-now-pay-later loans or credit cards to fund purchases.
  • Feeling temporary euphoria during purchase followed by deep guilt or anxiety.

🌿 Financial & Coping Restructuring

Enforce a 48-hour cooling-off rule before completing online purchases, delete saved credit cards from browsers, and seek professional budget counseling.
