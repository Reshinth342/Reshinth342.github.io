🧠 The Neuroscience of Social Validation & Algorithms

Social media platforms leverage our evolutionary need for social belonging. Features like infinite scroll, notification badges, and algorithmically curated content generate micro-spikes of dopamine combined with fear of missing out (FOMO).

📊 4 Stages of Social Media Overuse

1

Casual Connection

Using social apps to message friends and share updates.

2

Metric Dependence

Checking likes and comments repeatedly for emotional validation.

3

Doomscrolling

Scrolling feeds for hours despite feeling anxious or exhausted.

4

Compulsive Reflex

Opening apps automatically without conscious decision.
