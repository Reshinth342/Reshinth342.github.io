🧠 Neurobiology of Substance Dependency

Substances interact directly with endogenous opioid receptors, dopamine transporters, or serotonin pathways. High chemical flooding causes rapid down-regulation of brain receptors, leading to severe tolerance, physiological dependency, and intense post-acute withdrawal symptoms (PAWS).

📊 4 Stages of Substance Use Progression

1

Prescription / Initial Use

Using medication for pain or recreation out of curiosity.

2

Increased Tolerance

Requiring higher doses to achieve baseline pain or mood relief.

3

Compulsive Seeking

Doctor shopping or acquiring substances outside prescribed channels.

4

Physical Dependency

Using continuously to avoid acute physical withdrawal illness.

🚨 Crisis Safety & Harm Reduction

Harm reduction saves lives. Carrying Naloxone (Narcan)—a life-saving overdose reversal nasal spray—and never using alone are vital safety measures. In an emergency, call 911 immediately. Good Samaritan laws protect individuals seeking help.
