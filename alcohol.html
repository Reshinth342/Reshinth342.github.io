🍷 Alcohol Addiction & Dependence

Alcohol Use Disorder (AUD) is a chronic medical condition involving brain reward alterations, physical tolerance, and persistent cravings.

🧠 Neurobiological Deep Dive: How Alcohol Alters the Brain

Alcohol acts as a central nervous system depressant. It enhances the inhibitory neurotransmitter GABA (causing relaxation and slurred speech) while suppressing the excitatory neurotransmitter glutamate. Over time, the brain compensates by suppressing natural GABA production, resulting in heightened anxiety, physical shakiness, and insomnia when alcohol wears off.

📊 The 4 Stages of Alcohol Addiction Progression

1

Social & Experimental

Drinking socially to unwind or celebrate. Control remains intact.

2

Regular Coping

Relying on alcohol to manage stress, sleep, or social anxiety.

3

Problematic Misuse

Tolerance rises. Hiding drinking, experiencing hangovers at work.

4

Physical Dependence

Compulsive drinking to prevent withdrawal tremors or panic.

⚠️ Medical Safety Warning: Severe Alcohol Withdrawal

Sudden cessation of heavy long-term alcohol use can cause severe withdrawal complications (Delirium Tremens, seizures, extreme tremors). Do not attempt unsafe unmonitored home detox if you have heavy physical dependence. Please seek immediate professional medical support or hospital supervision.
