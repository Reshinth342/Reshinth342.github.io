Music & Recovery: Emotional Regulation

Music therapy stimulates natural endorphin release, lowers acute cortisol stress, and provides a powerful non-toxic tool for managing cravings and emotional triggers.

Featured Recovery Anthem

"One Step at a Time" — Anthem of Resilience

A song about overcoming addiction, taking recovery one hour at a time, and building emotional strength.

🧠 Brain Wave Entrainment & Cravings

Listening to music with slow tempos (60–80 BPM) promotes Alpha brainwave states (8–12 Hz), which reduce sympathetic nervous system arousal and assist in riding out acute 15-minute craving surges.
