Actionable Recovery

Comprehensive Addiction Recovery Guide

Evidence-based strategies for starting recovery, managing cravings, identifying triggers, building healthy daily routines, and supporting loved ones.

1. Starting the Recovery Journey

Recovery begins with acknowledging that current behavioral or substance patterns are causing harm and making a conscious commitment to change. Recovery is rarely a linear path; setbacks can occur and are opportunities to adjust your strategy.

2. Managing Intense Cravings

Cravings are temporary neurochemical surges that naturally peak and fall within 15 to 20 minutes. Useful craving techniques include:

  • Urge Surfing: Observing craving sensations non-judgmentally as waves that will pass.
  • HALT Check: Checking if you are Hungry, Angry, Lonely, or Tired.
  • Environment Change: Immediately stepping out of the physical space triggering the craving.

3. Relapse Prevention & Handling Setbacks

A relapse is a signal that your coping plan or support system needs adjustment. It does NOT mean recovery has failed. Immediately reconnect with your support contact, review your trigger journal, and resume your recovery routine.

4. Supporting a Loved One

Offer calm empathy rather than blame. Set healthy personal boundaries, avoid enabling behaviors, and encourage professional medical support.
